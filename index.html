    What changes with age

    Children5 to 12 years
    The aim is steady growth, never weight loss. Milk or yogurt, beans or lentils, eggs or cheese and fruit every day, an hour of outdoor play, and fewer cookies, chips and sugary drinks. Pediatricians read a child's BMI on a growth chart for their age and sex.
    Teens13 to 17 years
    Growth spurts need extra calories, iron and calcium. Do not skip breakfast or crash diet. Sport and strength work build healthy bodies at this age. If worry about food or weight starts to take over, talk to a doctor.
    Adults18 to 59 years
    A loss of about half a kilo a week is slow enough to keep off. Protein at every meal protects muscle. Walk daily, lift or use body weight twice a week, and sleep about seven hours, because poor sleep raises hunger.
    Seniors60 years and above
    Keep muscle. Any loss should be slow and gentle, and more protein helps: beans, yogurt, eggs, fish, cheese. Watch calcium, vitamin D and B12, and check with your doctor if you take regular medicines.
